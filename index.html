Tips for overnight use
  • Tone tilts each noise darker or brighter. Double-tap the slider to return to natural.
  • Width sends different noise to each ear. Wide feels spacious in headphones; mono can feel calmer on one speaker.
  • Drift adds a very slow, barely-there movement. Leave it at still for perfectly steady noise.
  • The noise is generated live and never repeats, so there's no loop for your ear to latch on to.
  • Phone speakers can't reproduce deep bass, so brown noise sounds thinner on them. Headphones or a bedside speaker bring it out.
  • Add Sleep Tones to your home screen (browser menu → Add to Home screen / Install app). It then opens full-screen and works offline.
  • Sound keeps playing with the screen off. If it stops after a while, set your browser's battery use to Unrestricted (Settings → Apps → your browser → Battery).
  • The moon button dims the screen to near-black with a quiet clock. Tap anywhere to return.
